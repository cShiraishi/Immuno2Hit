Immuno2Hit

Screening across the three arms of immune regulation — innate, adaptive and tolerance. One SMILES per line, up to 100 per run. Each target applies the consensus rule defined in its own paper.

This is the first gate of each paper's funnel, not a verdict. Clearing it means a compound is worth carrying forward to the applicability-domain, structural-alert, diversity and ADMET filters — which is where most survivors are eliminated.
